# Apple Liquid Glass Guidance

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials

- Liquid Glass is a dynamic material for functional controls and navigation.
- Do not use it in the content layer.
- Use standard materials for content/background structure.
- Use `regular` or `clear` only when the underlying visual context warrants it.
- Standard system components should be preferred over custom effects.
- Respect reduced transparency and increased contrast behavior.
- Limit simultaneous custom glass effects and preserve legibility.
